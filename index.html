About Us

Excellence in Christian Girls' Education

Set in Rongai at the heart of Nakuru County, Vanessa Grant Girls' School is a happy, disciplined Christian school which helps girls achieve very high standards in all they do, so that they leave well equipped to become exceptional leaders in whatever field they choose to enter.

Our mission is to provide the very best in boarding education for girls in Kenya. We are a compassionate, focused and motivated community where truth, integrity and humility are central to everyday living, following the example set by Christ in the Anglican tradition.

Principal's Welcome

Nurturing the Leaders of Tomorrow

“At Vanessa Grant Girls' School, we are dedicated to nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.”

At VGGS we pride ourselves on providing a nurturing and enriching environment where every student can thrive. Our amenities enable innovative learning experiences that foster academic excellence and personal growth, and our well-trained teachers are passionate about empowering girls to reach their highest potential.

Beyond academics, our guidance and counselling services prioritise each student's emotional and mental wellbeing, and our diverse co-curricular programme helps girls discover and develop their talents. As we deliver the Competency-Based Curriculum, we remain committed to a holistic education that prepares resilient, confident young women for the future.

Madam Jacklyne AmwayiPrincipal, Vanessa Grant Girls' School

Parents' Corner

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Vanessa Grant Girls' School located?

We are in Rongai, Nakuru County (P.O. Box 14-20108, Rongai). Use the map link in the footer for directions.

Is VGGS a boarding school?

Yes. VGGS is a girls' boarding school with a dedicated boarding section and catering department caring for students throughout the term.

What is the school's faith tradition?

VGGS is a Christian community that promotes the Christian faith in the Anglican tradition, served by an active chaplaincy at Bethel Chapel.

How do I apply for admission?

Call 0727 094 386 or submit the enquiry form above. You will be taken through the admission requirements and interview dates, then complete the online application.

Are bursaries or scholarships available?

Yes. VGGS runs a bursaries and scholarships programme, and students can also be supported through the Vanessa Grant Trust sponsorship scheme.

What co-curricular activities are offered?

Clubs and activities include robotics, music, art, drama, netball, skating, scouts, St. John's Ambulance, debate and more, coordinated by the Department of Co-curricular Activities.

Admissions enquiries are open. Talk to us today.
